# Painted Remembrance artwork

Created on 7 November 2026 using the built-in image generation tool. The active transparent PNG is retained at `dist/assets/seasons/poppy-bold.png`. It uses the initial generation below, selected again for the larger composition after Philip found the reduced-size meadow too subtle. The later edit remains part of the design history and its project PNG was superseded. The preparation and credits helpers preserve this file. The other sixteen treatments are regenerated from `scripts/seasonal-art.py`.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent botanical artwork for the bottom edge of an elegant British Classics department digital display. Create a very wide landscape 3:1 composition intended to run edge to edge below content. All the painting stays in the LOWEST QUARTER of the canvas, with the upper three quarters genuinely transparent and empty. Subject: an airy, naturalistic meadow of red remembrance poppies, thin charcoal and sage stems, a few seed heads. Sophisticated contemporary editorial botanical illustration, fine gouache and watercolour on translucent tissue, subtle petal veins and slight dry-brush grain. Beautifully varied small flowers arranged in asymmetrical loose clusters, open gaps, layered fine stems. Deep crimson and vermilion petals, near-black centres, muted grey-green stems. The flowers must be visibly botanically observed rather than geometric symbols. A few closer flowers at the far left and far right, centre low and sparse. Premium art-book endpaper, restrained and thoughtful mood. The overall image should composite naturally over dark navy #101522 with no painted background and no white halo. No text, labels, frame, ribbon or logo. Use actual transparent background. No repeated identical flowers, no cartoon petals, no sticker outlines, no clip art, no perfectly uniform spacing. Keep the tallest flower within the bottom quarter, preserve open space above.

## Final composition edit

Revise the supplied transparent botanical artwork for a very shallow digital display edge. Preserve its refined naturalistic painted poppies, translucency, colours and texture. Change the composition and scale decisively: make every flower and stem approximately ONE THIRD its current size. Arrange the much smaller poppies in an airy, loose, asymmetrical low field stretching across the entire wide canvas. Every painted pixel must be contained within the LOWEST 18 PERCENT of the image. The top 82 percent must be genuinely transparent and completely empty. No large flowers, no tall flowers at either end. Maximum flower head diameter about 2 percent of the total image width. Keep clusters, seed heads and open spaces varied with no identical repetitions. Use natural scarlet/crimson poppies and charcoal sage-green fine stems. No text, no line borders, no logos. Preserve actual transparency, with a clean alpha edge and no white or coloured halo. This is intended to be a thin sophisticated contemporary art-book botanical treatment below a page's content.

## 7 November: superseded in the active design

Philip selected flowing strands, flat stylised poppies, movement speed 1.6 and height 18–20 per cent. The local display now uses code-drawn motion at 20 per cent height, with matching generated SVG fallbacks. The painted PNG is retained as a historical asset and is no longer selected.
